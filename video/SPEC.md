# Rummage demo video, option B: "From a sentence to a hiding spot"

GOAL:    A judge finishes knowing that Lux3D's articulation is the whole game (every drawer, door and lid
         they saw was generated and given working joints by Lux3D) and wants to play it.
OUTPUT:  HyperFrames, 1920 x 1080, 30 fps, MP4.
TIME:    90 s x 30 = 2700 frames. Music: the game's own synthesized songs. Title theme at 112 BPM
         (1 beat = 16.07 frames) for 0–33.5 s, the workshop song at 124 BPM for the gameplay (33.5–82 s),
         title theme again under the end card.
LOOK:    The game's own Comic Ink material, nothing new:
         ground #1d2a9e (the game's blue), ink #14121f, cream #fff6e6 cards, yellow #ffc82e,
         one accent orange #f0542a, used only on the badge of the current step;
         display face Lilita One (the game's), body Nunito 800;
         cards: cream, 6 px ink border, 22 px radius, hard 8 px ink offset shadow, no blur, rotated -1.5°;
         badges: orange, white Lilita with a 2 px ink stroke, rotated -3° (the game's .badge).
         Footage is real gameplay recorded frame by frame from the live build; never re-coloured or blurred.
OBJECTS: the wizard's chest of drawers (w-drawers): its real prompt, motion prompt, joints (3 prismatic,
         0–0.226 m), timings (generated 118 s, articulated 145 s) from app/public/lux3d-manifest.json.
MOTION:  cards arrive with back.out(1.6) in 0.45 s from 40 px below with -6° → -1.5° rotation (playful
         spring, the game's feel); exits power3.in 0.25 s; prompt text types at 38 chars/s;
         counters count up over 0.9 s expo.out; labels pop with back.out(2) 0.3 s, 90 ms stagger.
SHOTS:
  0.0–7.0   hook: Grand Library, every joint opens in a wave. Logo slams at 2.4 s (scale 1.6→1, back.out).
            Line at 4.2 s: "Every drawer, door and lid opens."
  7.0–10.5  type on blue: "Every piece was generated by AI" → "from one sentence." (Lux3D highlighted).
  10.5–18.5 step 1: turntable of the chest of drawers (right 62%); card left: badge "1 · Text to 3D",
            the real prompt typing, chip "Lux3D G1-Turbo · 118 s".
  18.5–27.5 step 2: drawers slide out, then pull apart. Card: badge "2 · Articulation", the real motion
            prompt typing; at 5.6 s three joint labels pin to the drawers: "prismatic · slides 0.23 m".
  27.5–33.5 step 3: the same chest in the game, tapped, key inside, "Found it!". Badge
            "3 · Every joint is a hiding spot". Tap mark at the click point.
  33.5–43.5 numbers over a 4-room montage (2.5 s each): 55 pieces / 107 joints / 43 props & items / 10 levels.
  43.5–82.3 gameplay, a caption badge per mechanic: decoys, padlocks, mimics, night + secrets,
            hint + bet, beat the clock, find every key.
  82.3–90.0 end card on blue: logo, "Play free in your browser", rummage-game.vercel.app,
            "Built with Aholo Lux3D", github.com/emmaGH1/rummage. Last line held ≥ 2 s.
SOUND:   music bed at about -18 LUFS under the game's own sound effects, logged during capture and
         placed at their exact frames (slide, creak, key, chomp, unlock, win...). No voiceover.
BANNED:  dark void grounds, glows, gradients, glass cards, everything centred, fade-and-slide-up on every
         element, corner HUD decoration, three cards in a row, stock music, invented numbers,
         anything that implies a feature we didn't build.
QA:      2.5 (logo landed), 8.5 (question readable), 14 (prompt half typed, model visible),
         25 (labels on drawers), 30 (tap + key), 38 (counter), 52 (padlock), 63 (night), 70 (clue/bet),
         86 (end card readable).
