The Useless Box

It has one job: turning itself off.

Unpacking a box that wants to be packed…

How it was made

Both pieces of furniture were generated by Aholo Lux3D text-to-3D from one sentence each, then given working joints by Lux3D articulation from a one-line motion prompt. The lid, the switch, and the cabinet's doors and drawer move on the joints in the URDF that Lux3D returned. Nothing was rigged by hand.

The glove hand is a Lux3D text-to-3D prop. Its path, the box's moods and the sounds are code.

Made in an afternoon for the Manycore Aholo Global 3D AI Hackathon by the team behind Rummage.
